FAQ

Before you join.

What is the difference between Card+ and Card 3?

Card+ is a monthly subscription with a three-month minimum term. Card 3 is a prepaid 90-day pass.

How is Card+ billed?

The first payment covers two months. Monthly charges begin in month three.

Can I buy a membership for a child?

A parent or guardian registers and purchases for members under 18.

Is this the same as Loyalty Club?

No. Membership provides the access described in your plan. Loyalty Club is a separate programme.
