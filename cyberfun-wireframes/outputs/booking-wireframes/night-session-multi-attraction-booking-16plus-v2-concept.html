Your friends. Your games. Your night. Bring everyone together for competitive challenges and shared moments, from 18:00 to 22:00 for guests 16+.

Package overview

Two attractions. One great night out.

Go head-to-head at Lasertag, test your reflexes at Pixel or finish with songs in a private Karaoke room. Choose a combination that suits your group and make an ordinary evening something to remember.

Pick your package, guest count and the start time for your first attraction. Your second attraction follows flexibly, so the evening keeps moving without another fixed appointment.

FAQ

Before your night out.

When are Nighttime Packages available?

During Adult Time, from 18:00 to 22:00.

Who can join?

Nighttime Packages are for guests aged 16 and over.

Do both attractions have a fixed time?

You choose the start time for the first attraction. The second follows flexibly without a separate fixed slot.

How is the package priced?

The package has a price per person. Your selected package and guest count determine the total.

Can we book a company celebration?

For larger groups and tailored events, explore Corporates & Groups.
