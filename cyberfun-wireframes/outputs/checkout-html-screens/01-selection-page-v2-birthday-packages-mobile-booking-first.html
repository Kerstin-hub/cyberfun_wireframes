Cyberfun Birthday Parties

Big birthday energy, easy for parents. Choose a 2-hour or 3-hour birthday package with ticket, cake, drink and safety socks included, then add food, decorations or extra game Tokens in one flow.

Book party View details

Intro

Hosted parties with attractions, table time and extras in one package.

Cyberfun Birthday Parties are built for kids and teens who want more than cake and a table. Every birthday package includes the core party essentials, while Cyberfun and Cybermegafun add Tokens for extra games.

Families can choose the date, time, duration, package, participants and extras without jumping between separate booking steps.

Party room image

How it works

Pick the package, then build the party around your group.

The booking module keeps the core choices together: date, time, duration, package, participants and add-ons.

  • Two or three hours of party time
  • Ticket, cake, drink and socks included in every birthday package
  • Optional food, decorations, Flex upgrade and extra game Tokens

Good to know

Clear rules before families book.

  • Minimum 5 party guests
  • Birthdays can be booked until 18:00
  • Safety socks are included in every birthday package
  • Parents can add food packages, decorations and extra Tokens during booking
  • Looking for an adult party? Go to Adult Parties.
