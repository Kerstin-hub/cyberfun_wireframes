FAQs

Good to know.

What is the Cyberfun Loyalty Club?

A club for returning guests, with benefits and offers accessible through your Cyberfun account. Full programme details will be published here.

Is Loyalty Club the same as Membership?

No. Membership offers subscription plans. Loyalty Club is a separate programme for returning guests.

Where can I see my benefits?

Log in to your Cyberfun account to access your Loyalty Club information.

Which visits qualify for rewards?

Eligible bookings and reward conditions will be listed in the final club terms.

How do I use a club offer?

Each offer will show its own redemption instructions and conditions in your account.
