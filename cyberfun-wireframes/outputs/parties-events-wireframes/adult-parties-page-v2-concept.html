How it works

Select guests, Tokens and up to five attraction sessions.

The booking flow keeps Adult Night package, participants, included Tokens, missing Tokens and session times together before registration and payment.

Step 1

Choose Adult Night package

Guests select Basic, Fun or Mega Fun, each with a fixed Token amount included.

Step 2

Pick up to five sessions

Karaoke, Lasertag, Pixel, Darts, VR, MiniGolf and ValoFloor can be combined after 19:00H.

Step 3

See Token balance

The summary shows included Tokens, required Tokens and any missing extra Tokens.

Step 4

Register and pay

The booking is completed through the separate registration and payment screens.
