FAQ

Before you get together

What can we play together?

Try Pixel, Lasertag, Darts or Karaoke with friends, or choose an hour or two on the trampolines. Group minimums depend on the attraction and date.

Can under-16s visit in the evening?

Guests under 16 can visit before 19:00. From 19:00, visits are for ages 16 and over.

Can we celebrate a birthday?

Yes. Check out the birthday packages for jumping or games, plus cake and a drink.

How can our school plan a trip?

Use the School Groups inquiry form to share your group size and preferred date.
