Ticket overview

Plan an easy daytime visit.

The Family Time Ticket is the entry point for daytime visits at Cyberfun. Guests choose the date, select who is joining and can add Tokens or essentials before continuing to registration and payment.

Family Time runs until 19:00. Online ticket sales end at 18:00, while specific daytime attraction sessions are chosen on site during the visit.

Ticket experience image

What is included

Entry first, extras only if needed.

The product page keeps the ticket easy to understand: start with admission, then add socks, Tokens or family value options depending on the visit.

  • Daytime access for Family Time
  • Trampoline and softplay access where applicable
  • Optional Tokens for extra games on site
