Intro

Private karaoke rooms for every kind of group.

Karaoke is the social pause button inside the Cyberfun mix: a private room where guests can sing, laugh, celebrate and reset between high-energy attractions. It works for birthdays during the day, teen groups, adult nights and company events, either as a standalone booking or as an add-on to a larger party package.

The experience should feel easy to understand before booking: choose your room, select 30 or 60 minutes, add it to your visit and arrive ready to play. The page needs to make the attraction clear quickly, then support guests with details, visuals, good-to-know information and FAQs.
