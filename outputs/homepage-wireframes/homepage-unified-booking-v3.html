FAQ

Good to know before you book.

What is the difference between Family Time and Adult Time?

Adults can book during Family Time. Children up to 15 cannot book Adult Time. The available booking options change with the selected date and time.

Can I prebook attraction times during Family Time?

Daytime packages include an arrival time, but individual attraction times are arranged on site.

How do two-attraction packages work?

You choose a start time for the first attraction. The second attraction follows automatically and does not need another time selection.

What is included in birthday packages?

Birthday packages include tickets, cake, a drink and socks. Selected packages also include Tokens for additional games.

Can company and private groups book?

Yes. Corporate, school and private groups can choose dedicated formats or request a tailored visit.
